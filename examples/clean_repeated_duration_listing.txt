Barcelona Old City & Boulevard Walk

Explore two contrasting sides of central Barcelona on a small-group walking experience through the old city and one of its busiest pedestrian avenues.

Your guide will begin with the narrow lanes of the historic quarter, where traces of the Roman settlement sit alongside medieval buildings and civic squares. Along the route, hear how the city developed over different periods while passing landmarks, hidden courtyards, and local gathering places.

The second half of the walk follows Barcelona's lively central boulevard. Your guide will introduce several cultural landmarks, markets, public artworks, and stories connected with everyday life in the city.

The experience lasts approximately 2 hours and is designed for a small group, allowing time for questions along the way.

Highlights:
- Explore medieval streets and historic squares
- Learn about Barcelona's Roman and medieval past
- Walk along a famous central boulevard
- See markets, public art, and notable architecture
- Get local recommendations from your guide

- Local recommendations
