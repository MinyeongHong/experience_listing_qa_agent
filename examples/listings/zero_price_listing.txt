Barcelona Historic Boulevard Private Walk

Take a guided walk through one of Barcelona's best-known central avenues and explore how the area developed from the edge of the old city into a major public space.

During the walk, your guide will introduce the different sections of the boulevard and explain how their character changed over time. The route passes historic buildings, a traditional market, a theatre district, public artworks, and several streets connecting the old city with the waterfront.

The experience lasts approximately 3 hours. Along the way, there will be several stops for historical commentary and time for questions.

This experience is free to join. There is no charge for participation.

Meeting point: Plaça de Catalunya.

Included:
- Private local guide
- Historical commentary
- 3-hour guided walking tour

Not included:
- Food and drinks
