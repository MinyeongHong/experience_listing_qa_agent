Barcelona Market Discovery Walk

Explore a traditional Barcelona market and nearby streets with a local guide.

The guided experience lasts approximately 2 hours.

Guests may choose to purchase snacks from market vendors during the tour. Most snacks cost between €3 and €8, but purchases are optional and are not included in the experience.

Meeting point: Mercat de Sant Antoni.

Included:
- Local guide
- Market visit

Not included:
- Food purchases
- Hotel pickup
